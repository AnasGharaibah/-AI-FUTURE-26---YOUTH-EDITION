# **Nabd Tech Employee Handbook 2026**

#### **1. Working Hours**

Core working hours are 9:00 to 17:00, Sunday to Thursday. Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

# **2. Annual Leave**

Full-time employees receive 21 days of paid annual leave per year. After 5 years of service this increases to 28 days. Unused leave of up to 7 days may be carried over to the next year.

## **3. Salary Bands**

Monthly salary bands in Jordanian Dinars (JOD):

| Level | Title | Min (JOD) | Max (JOD) |
|-------|-----------------|-----------|-----------|
| L1 | Junior Engineer | 700 | 1,000 |
| L2 | Engineer | 1,000 | 1,600 |
| L3 | Senior Engineer | 1,600 | 2,400 |
| L4 | Lead Engineer | 2,400 | 3,300 |

{2}------------------------------------------------

## **4. Training Budget**

Each employee has an annual training budget of 500 JOD for courses, certifications and conferences. Requests go to the HR portal at least 14 days before the event.
