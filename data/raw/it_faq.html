Nabd Tech IT FAQ

How do I reset my password?

Go to sso.nabd.example and click "Forgot password". Passwords expire every 90 days.

What is the Wi-Fi network?

Staff use NABD-STAFF. Guests use NABD-GUEST, which is valid for 24 hours.

Who do I contact for laptop issues?

Open a ticket at helpdesk.nabd.example or call extension 4200.
